Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Spoofed is provided by Yash Pattekar. This policy covers the Spoofed iPhone app, Spoofed Helper, and this setup website.

Information used on your devices

Spoofed uses location access to show your position and recenter the map. Selected locations, trip plans, settings, and simulation state are processed on your device. The app stores pairing credentials and trip information locally to provide its features. Spoofed does not operate a server that receives your location, routes, or pairing credentials.

Computer setup and the local connection

Spoofed Helper accesses the connected iPhone’s identifiers and pairing information to establish a trusted connection and transfer pairing credentials over USB. It does not upload this information to us. The computer retains its normal Apple device trust record. The helper does not save a separate copy of the remote pairing credentials.

The app’s local VPN configuration connects to developer services on your iPhone to simulate location. It is not an internet privacy VPN and does not send your browsing traffic to a server operated by us.

Apple services

Maps, place searches, and road directions use Apple MapKit. Requests such as search text and route endpoints are handled by Apple’s services under Apple’s Maps privacy information. Apple may also process App Store and diagnostic information according to your device settings and its privacy policies.

Advertising and analytics

Spoofed does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

Website and downloads

This website and helper downloads are hosted by GitHub. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to this website.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond and troubleshoot, and retain them only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Email is processed by our email provider, Gmail. Do not send pairing credentials or passwords. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Your choices and deletion

You can change Location Services and Local Network permissions in iPhone Settings. Stop an active simulation before removing pairing or deleting the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; copies in device backups may remain according to your backup settings. The VPN configuration and computer trust records are managed separately through device and computer settings. We cannot access or remotely delete information stored only on your devices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our privacy practices change. For privacy questions or support, contact yashpattekar@gmail.com.
