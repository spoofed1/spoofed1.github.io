LET’S GET CONNECTED

Set up Spoofed

  1. 1
    Open Spoofed on your iPhone

    Go to Settings → Receive from Computer. Keep that screen open and your phone unlocked.

  2. 2
    Connect with a USB cable

    Use Chrome or Edge on your computer. Close other apps that are using the phone.

  3. 3
    Connect and approve Trust

    Choose your iPhone when asked. We’ll create the pairing and send it straight into Spoofed.

Checking this browser…

First-time setup needs USB on iOS 26. After setup, use Spoofed with LocalDevVPN on your phone.

A little help, if you need it.

Which computers work?

Use a current desktop Chrome or Edge browser with WebUSB support. Safari and iPhone browsers cannot run this setup. USB access varies by operating system and driver. If another app or driver owns the phone’s USB interface, the browser may be unable to connect.

Does anything get uploaded?

No pairing file, device identifier, or location is sent to a server. Pairing and USB transfer run in this browser. This page uses no analytics, accounts, or credential storage. GitHub hosts the website files.

The phone isn’t connecting

Unlock it, check the cable, close Xcode and other device tools, then disconnect and reconnect. Approve Trust if asked. On Windows or Linux, driver or permission setup may be needed. Don’t change drivers blindly; manual pairing remains available inside Spoofed.

Is this tested on my phone?

This setup is a preview. The app and browser engine are built and checked, but physical USB pairing must still be verified on your phone and computer. A transfer message means the file was sent; Spoofed confirms whether it was accepted.
