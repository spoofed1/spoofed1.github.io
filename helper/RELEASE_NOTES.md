Spoofed Helper creates remote pairing credentials and sends them directly into the Spoofed iPhone app over USB.

1. Download and open the helper for your computer.
2. On iPhone, open Spoofed → Setup → Receive from Computer.
3. Connect and unlock the iPhone, approve Trust, then click **Set up iPhone**.
4. Wait for **Pairing received** in Spoofed. Unplug, connect LocalDevVPN, and return to the map.

Mac: universal Apple silicon/Intel app, macOS 12+. Unzip before opening.
Windows: portable x64 application, Windows 10/11. Apple Devices (or compatible Apple Mobile Device USB support) must be installed.

This is a preview. Automated build/protocol checks are included; physical iPhone pairing and location simulation still need device acceptance testing. This release is not Apple-notarized or signed with a Windows publisher certificate; operating systems may warn or block opening it. Checksums are provided for download verification.

The iPhone app must already be installed with the Receive from Computer feature. The latest app also confirms acceptance back to the helper. Older compatible receivers show delivery only; check confirmation on the phone. No account or uploads. The computer retains its normal Apple trust record; remote pairing is transferred without saving a separate desktop copy.

`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES-macOS.txt` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES-Windows.txt` includes dependency licenses. Source and pinned dependencies are included in this repository.
